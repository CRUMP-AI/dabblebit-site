Common questions

You start each session with 5 hearts. A wrong answer costs one. Run out and you'll need to wait for hearts to refill or practice earlier lessons to earn them back. Hearts keep things exciting — they're never sold for real money in v1.

Finish at least one lesson a day to keep your streak alive. Your streak counts the days in a row you've shown up — Puddles gets very excited about long ones.

JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go, Rust, and SQL unlock with Dabblebit Plus, which is coming soon. The full Python course is free forever — no payment, no trial, no catch.

We're sorry — in v1 your progress lives only on your device, so reinstalling starts you fresh. Cloud sync with accounts is on the roadmap, and when it lands, your progress will follow you anywhere.

Dabblebit is coming to iOS and Android phones and tablets. It needs an internet connection the very first time you use write-and-run coding (to download the Python runtime); lessons themselves work offline after that.

Please tell us! Use the "Report a bug" button above and include the language, level, and lesson (they're shown at the top of every lesson screen). We take lesson accuracy personally.
