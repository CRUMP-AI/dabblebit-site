Privacy Policy

The short version: we don't want your data.

Effective: October 2026

Dabblebit is made by Clever Crump. This policy explains what the Dabblebit app (and this website) do with your information. The honest summary: almost nothing.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts. Dabblebit v1 has no sign-up, no login, no profiles.
  • No analytics. We don't track how you use the app, and there are no analytics SDKs inside it.
  • No advertising. There are no ads in Dabblebit and no ad SDKs collecting anything.
  • No location, contacts, or device identifiers. The app never asks for them.

What stays on your device

Your learning progress — lessons completed, XP, streaks, hearts, badges — is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else. The tradeoff: if you delete the app or switch phones, your progress starts over. Cloud sync is planned for a future version.

One thing that touches the network

Dabblebit's write-and-run coding feature runs real Python on your phone using the Pyodide runtime. On first use, the app downloads that runtime from the jsDelivr content delivery network (cdn.jsdelivr.net). That's a plain file download — no personal information is sent, and it only happens when you use the feature.

This website

If you join the launch list, we receive the email address you type in — only to email you about Dabblebit's launch. We don't share it, sell it, or add it to anything else. Reply to any launch email asking to be removed and you're gone.

Children

Dabblebit is designed to be friendly for learners of all ages, and because the app collects nothing, there's nothing to protect. Parents: the app has no chat, no accounts, and no way for strangers to contact your child through it.

Changes to this policy

If Dabblebit ever starts collecting anything (for example, when cloud sync arrives), we'll update this page and note what's changed before it happens.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email gregory.d.crump@gmail.com and a human (probably Greg) will answer.
