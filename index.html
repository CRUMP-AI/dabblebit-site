How it works

Teach first. Quiz second. Never guess.

Every lesson starts with a friendly teach card that explains the idea in plain English — so you always know before you're asked.

Step 01

Learn first

Short teach cards explain each concept like a friend would — with real code, why it matters, and zero jargon. You never face a question you weren't taught.

Step 02

Play

Multiple choice, tap-to-pair matching, fill-in-the-blank — and write-and-run coding where your actual code executes on your phone. Streaks and hearts keep it spicy.

Step 03

Hatch

Your buddy starts as a smooth little egg. Every level you clear cracks it a little more — finish the whole language and it hatches into your pal, Puddles.
